Keep your choices.

A tool can earn its place through useful output, clear costs and a handoff you can test. These examples help you compare approaches; the calculator does not contact any service.

Local, hosted or a mixture

What are you
actually buying?

A subscription to an app and a separately billed API can have different allowances. Compare the route you intend to use.

01

Use what you have

Own footage, an existing transcript, your own voice and local rendering can avoid generation API charges. Hardware, electricity and your time still have costs.

02

Pay per task

Hosted services may bill by tokens, audio minutes, image outputs, characters or generated seconds. Alternatives and retries can add up.

03

Swap with a test

Compare quality, privacy, rights, timing detail, output format and availability. Similar billing units do not make two services interchangeable.
