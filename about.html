What this workshop offers

Created by Luke Nathan Hayes / Strange But True / Aura of Intelligence, this site turns research into practical learning paths for music videos, educational films, shorts and a proposed Queens Council.

The material distinguishes existing source tools from proposed connections and a learner's own test results. It offers possible approaches rather than a promise that one workflow or vendor will suit every project.

Imagery and identity

The panoramic workshop, council discussion and spatial-storytelling scenes are original generated concept artworks made for this site with built-in image generation. They imagine possible creative spaces; they do not document real facilities or meetings.

The individual Queen illustrations and profiles come from Luke's existing 500 Queens project. Their identity, questions and limits are retained in the source map. The five-woman council scene is a broader concept, rather than a precise portrait of the selected panel.

The favicon is an original pixel-based infinity mark supplied as PNG and ICO files. Fonts use the device's installed families, so the site does not need a remote font request.

Your browser and your notes

The council brief and test records can save in this browser's local storage. They are not sent to an AI provider or a server by the workshop. Clearing the browser's stored data removes them. You can export your own copy.

The cost planner does arithmetic with published or user-entered rates. It does not request API keys, create subscriptions or purchase generation. The host may process ordinary request information according to its own terms. External source links take you to their respective services.

Source relationships

The design builds on the navigation, panoramic hero, palette and licence pattern of DaVinci Resolve Automations, alongside the creator-first approach of Explorer Music Video Lab. The research also inspected Infinity Engine, Herk Edit Kit, the earlier HyperFrames pipeline and Roast Room.

Local source checks on 30 October 2026 passed Herk's 11 behaviour tests and the earlier pipeline's five overlapping editing tests. Roast Room's offline viewer loaded its example sessions. These observations concern those inspected versions; they do not certify future integrations or every library card.

Blender 4.4 and Resolve 21 were identified on the research computer. Completing the production connections and testing the installed controls is the next practical exercise.
