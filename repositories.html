One repository or several?

Start with one
training repository.

This website brings lessons, imagery, costs and test records into one coherent place. The council and production software can develop in their existing repositories.

Keep together when

The material shares navigation, audience, visual identity and a release rhythm. A change to a lesson and its cost example is easier to review in one place.

Consider a split when

A runtime needs independent deployment, credentials, private data, different owners or its own release schedule. A separate adapter may then be useful.

More participants do not automatically call for more repositories. Serving real participants introduces questions about access, storage and support that are different from choosing a fictional council panel.

How this workshop is maintained

Make the learning
easy to revisit.

Update the source date when checking rates, retain asset provenance and keep new private recordings outside the public source.
