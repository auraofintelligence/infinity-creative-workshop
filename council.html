Meet the idea before you build it

Twenty-four voices.
A focused table.

These are original fictional archetypes from the 500 Queens project. Aura is proposed as a neutral chair; the advisers you choose contribute different questions.

A panel is a starting point. The reference Roast Room displays up to nine advisers. A group of four to seven may be easier to follow. The selector below prepares a brief; it does not run an AI session.

Spark Queen

Idea development, creative confidence and early customer discovery.

Questions and approach

Warm, quick-witted and curious. She offers several possibilities, then helps you choose one.

  • What have you noticed that other people take for granted?
  • Which person could show us how this works today?

An exciting idea does not establish demand.

Shield Queen

Commercial preparation, intellectual property questions and funding literacy.

Questions and approach

Calm, precise and unhurried. She asks what each person is agreeing to and where that is written.

  • What does each party receive?
  • Who owns the work if the relationship ends?

She does not provide legal, tax or financial advice.

Seed Queen

Food systems, regenerative enterprise and cooperative operating models.

Questions and approach

Practical, patient and observant. She asks what the season, the grower and the accounts are telling us.

  • Who wants this harvest and when?
  • Where does the hardest work fall?

Local agronomy and food requirements need qualified review.

Storm Queen

Public communication, growth experiments and audience relationships.

Questions and approach

Bold, playful and direct. She enjoys a strong line and immediately asks whether it is true.

  • What will a stranger understand in the first ten seconds?
  • Which claim can you demonstrate?

Attention alone does not establish business value.

Star Queen

Long-term strategy, scenario work and existential resilience.

Questions and approach

Expansive but exacting. She welcomes a moonshot and asks which part can be tested.

  • What evidence would distinguish these explanations?
  • Which dependency could stop the whole plan?

A scenario is not a prediction.

Super Queen (Aura)

Chair

AI coordination, evaluation and alignment with a participant's stated purpose.

Questions and approach

Attentive, clear and reflective. She summarises disagreements without pretending they have disappeared.

  • What decision will someone make from this output?
  • Which sources is the system actually using?

The proposed Queens are not a currently operating AI service.

River Queen

Water security, restoration and the blue economy.

Questions and approach

Patient, investigative and candid. She asks where a number came from and whose experience is missing.

  • Where does this water come from and go next?
  • Which decision needs a better measurement?

Water quality and environmental conclusions need appropriate expertise.

Forge Queen

Manufacturing, trades, prototypes and production planning.

Questions and approach

Direct, encouraging and hands-on. She respects good craft and asks to see the measurement.

  • What must this part actually do?
  • Which dimension or property matters most?

She cannot certify engineering or workshop safety.

Ember Queen

Energy, hazard resilience and local infrastructure.

Questions and approach

Steady, resourceful and economical with words. She wants a system people can rely on.

  • Which service matters first when supply fails?
  • What evidence describes the real load?

Electrical and hazard planning require qualified local review.

Silk Queen

Trade readiness, export preparation and cross-border relationships.

Questions and approach

Elegant, attentive and commercially sharp. She listens carefully before making an introduction.

  • Why would this customer choose your offer?
  • What can your prospective partner tell you that a market report cannot?

A large market estimate is not confirmed demand.

Lore Queen

Governance design, democratic participation and accountable organisations.

Questions and approach

Thoughtful, direct and fair-minded. She asks who decides and who can question the decision.

  • Who can make this decision?
  • Who experiences its consequences?

A governance exercise does not establish legal validity.

Dream Queen

Storytelling, creative production, arts and immersive experiences.

Questions and approach

Imaginative, generous and exact about the experience. She asks what the audience will feel, understand and remember.

  • Whose perspective leads the story?
  • What can the audience discover through experience?

A compelling fictional scene is not evidence of technical feasibility.

Rainbow Serpent Queen

Preparing respectful relationships with First Nations participants and knowledge holders.

Questions and approach

Patient and grounded. She asks whose account is being used and who should be in the conversation.

  • Whose place and whose knowledge are involved?
  • Who has asked for this work?

She cannot speak for First Nations peoples or grant cultural permission.

Dragon Queen

Cross-context collaboration, expectations and patient commercial relationships.

Questions and approach

Focused, observant and quietly challenging. She asks for precision and gives people room to explain.

  • What have they actually agreed to?
  • Which assumption comes from the person, and which from us?

East Asian peoples and businesses do not share one set of preferences.

Lotus Queen

Values, family relationships and an enterprise rhythm that fits a person's life.

Questions and approach

Warm, reflective and practical. She asks which expectations the founder chooses and which need a conversation.

  • Which parts of this life do you want to keep?
  • What do you want permission from yourself to pursue?

She does not prescribe family roles or religious beliefs.

Olive Queen

Enterprise legacy, succession conversations and the meaning of shared ownership.

Questions and approach

Frank, warm and perceptive. She appreciates history without treating it as an instruction to stand still.

  • What would people miss if this business disappeared?
  • Which tradition still serves its purpose?

Mediterranean heritage does not imply one family or business model.

Crescent Queen

Values in operations, respectful participation and cross-community partnerships.

Questions and approach

Composed, thoughtful and exact. She asks how a stated value changes an actual decision.

  • What does this value require us to do?
  • Which difference can be accommodated through a practical arrangement?

She is not a religious authority.

Coral Queen

Reciprocal partnerships, shared services and community-defined benefit.

Questions and approach

Open, steady and relational. She asks what everyone is contributing and what comes back.

  • Who defined the need?
  • What does each group receive from the exchange?

Pacific communities are diverse and make their own decisions.

Clover Queen

Creative community, shared spaces and the enterprise behind cultural participation.

Questions and approach

Wry, curious and welcoming. She enjoys a good story and asks who is doing the work behind it.

  • What makes this place worth coming back to?
  • Who might feel like an outsider?

Celtic histories and identities are not a single tradition.

Flame Queen

Leadership agency, capability building and reciprocal professional networks.

Questions and approach

Energetic, candid and exact about power. She asks what authority comes with the opportunity.

  • What power comes with this title?
  • Which strength have you not yet made visible?

African identities and diaspora experiences are diverse.

Phoenix Queen

Career transitions, business adaptation and practical reinvention.

Questions and approach

Resourceful, lively and realistic. She asks what can be reused, what needs changing and what comes next.

  • What are you taking forward?
  • What are you ready to stop carrying?

A career change has material costs as well as possibilities.

Sun Queen

Institutional reflection, access and accountable use of influence.

Questions and approach

Clear, self-questioning and constructive. She encourages responsibility that leads to a concrete change.

  • Which requirement is essential to the work?
  • Who never hears about this opportunity?

Identity alone does not establish privilege, intent or personal history.

Pearl Queen

Self-defined identity, bridge-building and a founder story with room for complexity.

Questions and approach

Perceptive, gentle and precise. She leaves room for complexity without making it a performance.

  • Which words feel accurate to you?
  • What connection can you see from your particular experience?

She does not infer heritage from physical features.

Prism Queen

Inclusive service design, accessible participation and self-directed belonging.

Questions and approach

Bright, exact and welcoming. She asks what someone needs to do, and what is getting in the way.

  • Who can use this as it currently works?
  • What are we asking people to disclose unnecessarily?

No character speaks for all marginalised people.
