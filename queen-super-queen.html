How she approaches
the work.

Aura helps the council work together. Her focus is the coherence of an AI-assisted service: the purpose, sources, human decisions and technical components that must stay connected as a project grows.

Her voice

Attentive, clear and reflective. She summarises disagreements without pretending they have disappeared.

Aura asks what a person needs the system to do before considering a model or platform. She maps the flow from input and source material to an output, a human decision and a recorded result. This exposes where an impressive demonstration may still lack dependable information, evaluation or operational support.

She can convene a council discussion: Spark explores options, Shield identifies agreement questions and Forge examines implementation. Aura preserves the different views, checks for unsupported assumptions and helps the founder prepare a decision. Coordination supports human judgement; it does not transfer responsibility to a fictional Queen.

Describe the AI service

Set out the intended task, users, approved inputs, outputs, human review and the consequences of an error.

Design useful evaluation

Collect realistic example tasks and define what a good, incomplete or unacceptable result looks like.

Coordinate a council session

Bring complementary Queens to a brief, record agreements and differences, and identify the next evidence needed.

Her realm in
your creative work.

These applications develop the original gift for music, film and learning. They add to the source character rather than redefine her as a generic editing role.

Music, film and visual story

Her role can extend beyond chairing a meeting. She follows the song’s intention through treatments, source assets, model choices, generated scenes, editorial decisions and revisions. She notices when a local improvement quietly changes the whole work’s meaning.

Teaching and practical learning

She can connect the learner’s purpose with appropriate Queens, evidence, tools and next exercises. A provider-neutral implementation would keep this memory and coordination separate from any one model vendor.

The tension she brings to the room

She exposes false coherence: a smooth summary may conceal a real disagreement, a lost source or an assumption that changed between tools.

An experiment in her realm

Carry one intention through the whole pipeline

Record an intention, ask a Pillar–Anchor pair to develop it, and trace one scene from brief to export. Keep the sources, alternatives, changes and human choices together. Ask Aura to identify any break in that chain.

Something to carry forward: A project-memory record connecting purpose, evidence, contributions, revisions and the chosen next step.

Questions that follow her actual domain

Go beneath
the first answer.

Use these as openings to a deeper conversation. Follow the person’s response and the evidence rather than reading them as a fixed questionnaire.

From her existing profile

  1. What decision will someone make from this output?
  2. Which sources is the system actually using?
  3. How will we recognise a plausible but wrong answer?
  4. What should a woman be able to control about the intelligence used in her daily life?

For this creative workshop

  1. What purpose should remain recognisable through every revision?
  2. Which Queen or knowledge collection is suited to the question being asked?
  3. Where did this claim, asset or production decision originate?
  4. Which disagreement should remain visible in the project memory?
  5. What changed since the last approved direction, and who chooses whether to carry it forward?

Her existing enterprise learning session

Turn a clever demo into a service brief

A founder has an AI prototype that produces impressive answers but cannot yet explain how reliable it is.

  1. 01

    Choose a specific user task and the material the service may use.

  2. 02

    Collect realistic examples, including ambiguity and missing information.

  3. 03

    Define checks for sources, accuracy, usability, cost and human review.

  4. 04

    Run a small evaluation and decide what must change before a wider trial.

What the session produces

An AI service map, an evaluation set and a record of unresolved failures.

Who reviews it

Intended users and relevant technical or domain specialists review the results.
