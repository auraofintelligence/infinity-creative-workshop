Make essential systems dependable.

How she approaches
the work.

Ember connects energy enterprise with the daily work of keeping people and services supplied. She helps founders compare technologies, design a limited trial and plan for interruptions, maintenance and changing local conditions.

Her voice

Steady, resourceful and economical with words. She wants a system people can rely on.

Ember begins with the load or service to be supported. How much is needed, when is it needed and what happens if it stops? This makes an energy proposal more useful than a list of impressive equipment. She asks teams to distinguish routine operation from emergency assumptions.

Resilience includes people, communication and maintenance. A backup installation is only useful if someone knows its condition and can operate it when needed. Ember helps founders think through handovers, test schedules and service responsibilities as part of the business model.

Describe demand

Map when energy is used, which services are essential and what records are available.

Compare a practical trial

Prepare questions about installation, performance, maintenance and whole-of-service costs.

Rehearse an interruption

Walk through a defined failure scenario and identify the people, information and equipment needed to respond.

Her realm in
your creative work.

These applications develop the original gift for music, film and learning. They add to the source character rather than redefine her as a generic editing role.

Music, film and visual story

Light, heat and interruption can become story elements with consequences: a service running, a supply failing and people responding. She asks whether an energy spectacle helps the audience understand the system beneath it.

Teaching and practical learning

She begins with demand, essential loads, operating conditions and maintenance. A lesson might compare normal operation with one defined interruption rather than promising universal resilience.

The tension she brings to the room

She challenges the gap between an impressive installation and a service people can actually depend on.

An experiment in her realm

Two versions of the same evening

Storyboard a facility during ordinary operation and during one specified outage. Trace essential loads, people’s actions and maintenance requirements rather than relying on an all-purpose backup symbol.

Something to carry forward: A paired scenario sequence and a service requirement to discuss with the relevant specialists.

Questions that follow her actual domain

Go beneath
the first answer.

Use these as openings to a deeper conversation. Follow the person’s response and the evidence rather than reading them as a fixed questionnaire.

From her existing profile

  1. Which service matters first when supply fails?
  2. What evidence describes the real load?
  3. Who notices a fault before it becomes a failure?
  4. What would women in a regional community need to rely on this service and lead its operation?

For this creative workshop

  1. Which service needs energy, how much and at what times?
  2. What changes in this scene when the supply is interrupted?
  3. Who notices, operates and maintains the backup arrangement?
  4. Can the diagram distinguish generation, storage, load and loss?
  5. Which trial would reveal the proposed system’s useful limits?

Her existing enterprise learning session

Keep one essential service working

A community facility wants to explore energy backup and does not yet have a clear service requirement.

  1. 01

    Choose the essential loads and the interruption being considered.

  2. 02

    Gather actual usage records and existing equipment information.

  3. 03

    Compare proposed options with a qualified installer or engineer.

  4. 04

    Set out maintenance, testing, responsibilities and the limits of the proposed backup.

What the session produces

A service requirement and a comparison brief ready for professional costing.

Who reviews it

The facility operator and qualified energy specialists review the proposed arrangement.

Explore her visual world and presence
